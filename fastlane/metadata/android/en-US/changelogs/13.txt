First F-Droid release (build 13).

* FOSS build: removed MSAL (OneDrive), ML Kit, Google Play Services dependencies
* QR scanning rewritten on ZXing: fixed portrait camera scanning, gallery scanning with dual-binarizer fallback and high-resolution retry
* OneDrive / Google Drive sync removed in this build; WebDAV sync fully available
* Native MDBX engine compiled from source during build
